## How a week works

Three consecutive evenings, two hours each, live. Then roughly two hours on your own
between blocks — three to four in Weeks 2 and 3, and that's a floor, not a cap.

Each week has one **Student Pack**. It carries that week's concepts, both labs, the
capstone component due before Monday, your homework, and the build-log prompts. If you
only open one file a week, open that one.

**The one rule that matters**: your capstone component for the week is committed to your
own repo *before* Monday's huddle. That's what the huddle is for. Nothing committed means
nothing to report.

## If you get stuck

Ask in the cohort channel before your pod's next check-in, not after. Everyone else is
hitting the same wall about ninety minutes after you do.
